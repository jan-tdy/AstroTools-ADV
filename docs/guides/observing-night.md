The site is Córdoba: longitude `-4.78`, latitude `37.89`, height `120 m`. The night is 29 October 2026; local time is UTC+1.

## Twilight and the Moon

Open [Sun, Moon & twilight](../reference/coordinates/coord_sunmoon.md).

1. Enter the site (**Mode** `Geodetic`).
2. **Start (UTC)** `2026-10-29 12:00`, **Display UTC offset [h]** `1`, so the tables show local time.
3. **Lines** (targets, for the Moon separation): `10.684 41.269` and `83.822 -5.391`.
4. Press **Compute**.

![Sun, Moon and twilight for Córdoba](../assets/guides/coords-sunmoon-night.png "Twilight, astronomical night and Moon for Córdoba, 29 October 2026.")

**Events** gives sunset 18:23:45, astronomical dusk 19:52:15, astronomical dawn 06:13:47 the next morning and sunrise 07:42:28. **Astronomical night** is `19:52:15 to 06:13:47`, 10.35 h. **Moon phase** reports a waning gibbous Moon; moonrise is 20:53:42, so the first hour of the dark sky is Moon-free, and **Moon separation** tells you how far each target is from it.

## Altitude of the targets

Open [Alt/Az & visibility](../reference/coordinates/coord_visibility.md).

1. Same site and the same two targets in **Lines**.
2. **Start (UTC)** `2026-10-29 18:00`; leave the 24 h duration and 10 min step.
3. Press **Compute** and open the **Altitude** tab.

![Altitude of M31 and the Orion Nebula](../assets/guides/coords-visibility-altitude.png "Altitude of M31 and the Orion Nebula on the night of 29 October 2026.")

**Rise / transit / set** shows M31 transiting at `22:30:17` UTC at 86.47 deg and the Orion Nebula at `03:21:53` UTC at 46.73 deg. M31 is nearly overhead, with airmass close to 1; Orion is best late in the night. Combine this with the astronomical night window above and pick the target for each part of the night.

For the first minutes of the track: M31 starts at altitude 39.07 deg, azimuth 63.4 deg, airmass 1.587 at 18:00 UTC.
